## 🔍 Key Learnings & Strategic Product Vision

### 1. Legacy Platform State vs. Future Product Mandate
- **Legacy State**: The platform was built specifically for My Analytics School's internal programs to train students as **Data Analysts** and **Business Analysts**.
- **New Product Vision**: Transform the platform into a **domain-agnostic, white-labelable B2B SaaS platform** that any educational institute (bootcamps, universities, coaching centers, vocational programs) can adopt and run independently.

### 2. Core PM Objectives Identified
- **Generic Architecture**: Remove hardcoded Data/Business Analytics assumptions across roadmaps, courses, tests, and CRM workflows.
- **Enhanced Student Journey**: Create flexible, customizable learning pathways, modular roadmaps, and domain-agnostic gamification.
- **Enhanced Mentor Journey**: Build configurable mentor evaluation scorecards, flexible slot types, and domain-agnostic mentor-student matching.
- **B2B Deployability**: Enable multi-tenancy, custom branding/white-labeling, configurable compliance document requirements, and plug-and-play LMS/Exam integrations.
