The 30-Second Pitch
MAS (My Analytics School) is an end-to-end student success platform. It takes a person from
being a sales lead, to an enrolled student in a batch, follows them through a structured learning
roadmap (live classes, video courses, and tests), keeps them motivated with XP, streaks, and badges,
connects them to mentors, and tracks them all the way to placement.
For the business, it's one system that runs the whole journey: marketing & sales →  enrollment →
teaching →  testing →  mentoring →  placement.
The Big Picture — one student's journey
flowchart LR
    L[Lead\nsomeone interested] --> A[Application\nthey sign up & pay]
    A --> B[Batch\ntheir cohort]
    B --> R[Roadmap\ntheir learning plan]
    R --> C[Mr Learn courses\nvideo lessons]
    R --> T[Mr Test exams\nassessments]
    R --> M[Live classes & mentors]
    C --> P[Progress, XP, badges]
    T --> P
    M --> P
    P --> J[Placement / job applications]
Everything in the platform is built around this ﬂow. The admin panel sets it up; the student
dashboard is what the learner sees.

Part 1 — The Student Experience
What a student sees on their dashboard
When a student logs in, their personal dashboard shows:
Their roadmap — a clear, ordered learning plan for their batch: what to study each week (video
courses, tests, live classes, assignments, projects, milestones), with progress on each item.
Progress ribbon — their Level (1 to 5), total XP (experience points), and their streak (how many
days in a row they've shown up), with an "on ﬁre today" indicator.
Badges — achievement awards they've earned (and ones still locked, with how to unlock them).
Courses & classes — the video courses and live sessions assigned to them.
Quizzes & AI classrooms — practice and interactive AI-led lessons.
Mentor calls — booking and joining 1-on-1 sessions with mentors.
Tokens — their balance of credits used to book mentor sessions.
Application & placement status — where they stand, including the "Pay After Placement"
agreement if applicable.
The Roadmap (what the student follows)
The roadmap is the heart of the student experience — an ordered list of learning steps for their
batch. Each step is one of:
Step type What it is
📘  Module A block of learning (often a video course)
📝  Test An assessment / exam
✏  Assignment Work to submit
🎥  Live Class A scheduled live session
🎙  Webinar A talk or special session
🚀  Project A hands-on project
🏁  Milestone A checkpoint or achievement marker
Steps are organized by week (or day/session/step, depending on the course). A student sees each
step, what's inside it (the actual video course or exam), their progress, and any session notes the team
added. Some tests are locked until the student ﬁnishes a required course ﬁrst.

Important nuance: Two students in the same batch can follow diﬀerent courses/roadmaps —
the platform supports per-student plans.
Gamiﬁcation — XP, Levels, Streaks, Badges (keeps students engaged)
This is a strong sales talking point: students stay motivated because the platform rewards them.
XP & Levels: Students earn points for logging in, completing modules, taking tests, ﬁnishing
mentor calls, etc. Points move them up through 5 levels.
Streaks: Showing up daily builds a streak; hitting milestones (every 7 days) earns bonus rewards.
Badges (6 starter achievements):
Badge How a student earns it
Explorer First login after onboarding
All Star Completing their full proﬁle
Momentum A 7-day streak
Achiever Passing their ﬁrst test
Collaborator Completing their ﬁrst mentor call
Scholar Finishing their ﬁrst course module
When a student unlocks a badge, they get a celebration moment and bonus XP. This drives daily
engagement and course completion.
Part 2 — The Admin Panel (how the team runs
it)
The admin panel is where the team builds courses, creates batches, manages leads, and connects the
testing and learning tools. Here's each major piece in plain English.
Leads & Sales CRM — turning interest into enrollment
A lead is a prospective student — someone who showed interest (via a form, a marketing campaign, a
vendor list, or a CSV upload). The Sales CRM is where the team manages all of them.
How a lead becomes a student: 1. Capture — leads come in from the website, marketing
campaigns, vendors, or uploads. 2. Distribute — leads are assigned to sales heads, then to

telecallers/counsellors. (Sales heads approve bulk assignments.) 3. Qualify — the team calls and
follows up. Leads are marked Hot / Warm / Cold, with follow-up reminders (Overdue / Today /
Upcoming). 4. AI calling ("Aarya") — an AI voice agent can call batches of leads automatically. Based
on how long the lead stays on the call, the system shows an AI-predicted interest chip
(Hot/Warm/Cold) to help the team prioritize. 5. Automation workﬂows — the team can build
automated nurture sequences: AI call →  if Hot, send WhatsApp/email →  wait →  follow up. 6. Convert
— an interested lead becomes a paid Application, then gets assigned to a Batch. 7. Pay After
Placement (MAS101) — for the ﬂagship program, students go through a digital agreement/MOU
signing ﬂow after payment.
Sales talking points: every lead is tracked, never lost; AI calling scales outreach; predicted-interest
helps reps focus on the hottest prospects; the whole funnel from ﬁrst contact to enrollment is in one
place.
Courses — the learning content
There are a few kinds of "course" in the system, each with a purpose:
Classic Course — the real program with a roadmap and modules. This is what enrolled students
follow.
New Courses (marketing catalog) — the polished course cards shown on the public website
(with pricing, highlights, badges-as-labels).
Course Plans — AI-generated multi-lecture courses used to create AI Classrooms (50–200 AI
lessons).
Modules — the building blocks of a course
A module is a teaching unit inside a course (it holds classes, quizzes, and assignments). Admins
create modules, order them, and can assign speciﬁc modules to a speciﬁc batch. Think of a course as
a book and modules as its chapters.
How a roadmap is created
The roadmap is built in the course editor: 1. Open the course and go to the Roadmap section. 2. Add
items one by one — pick a type (Module, Test, Live Class, etc.), give it a title, set its week, add a
description, thumbnail, links, and attachments. 3. Drag to reorder items; group them by week. 4.
Admins can create custom step types (with their own emoji + color) beyond the seven built-ins. 5.
Roadmaps can also be imported from a ﬁle (JSON) — one course at a time, or in bulk (up to 50
courses at once).

How to create a batch
A batch is a cohort of students going through a program together. To create one: 1. Click Create in the
Batches section. 2. Fill in: batch code (e.g. "mas101"), name, the course it runs (optional — it can be
independent), start/end dates, max students, pricing (oﬀer price + original/MRP price), GST settings,
required documents (Aadhaar, college ID, resume, etc.), a banner image, the batch lead, and
WhatsApp group links. 3. Save. Students get added to the batch through their applications, and
admins can even assign a diﬀerent course per student within the same batch.
Admins also get batch analytics — number of students, mentor connects, tokens used, revenue
generated, and per-student breakdowns with Excel export.
Mapping — how everything connects
"Mapping" is how the team wires the pieces together so a student gets the right content:
flowchart TD
    LEAD[Lead] --> APP[Application: student + batch + course + status]
    APP --> BATCH[Batch]
    BATCH --> COURSE[Course default - can be overridden per student]
    COURSE --> MOD[Modules: classes, quizzes, assignments]
    COURSE --> ROAD[Roadmap items by week]
    BATCH -. attach per roadmap item .-> MRL[Mr Learn courses]
    BATCH -. attach per roadmap item .-> MRT[Mr Test exams]
    BATCH -. session notes per item .-> NOTES[Roadmap notes]
In practice, in a screen called the Course Builder, the team takes each roadmap step and attaches
the actual Mr Learn video courses and Mr Test exams the batch's students should get — and can
require a course to be completed before a test unlocks.
Mr Test — the exams product
Mr Test is the testing/assessment side, powered by an external exam engine (EzExam). From the
admin panel the team can: - Browse available exams and see results. - Push MAS students into the
exam system as test-takers. - Sync exam results back into MAS so they show up on the student's
roadmap and feed badges/progress. - Attach an exam to a batch's roadmap (optionally locked behind
a required course).
In one line: Mr Test = the platform's exams and test-series, with results ﬂowing back into each
student's record.

Mr Learn — the video learning product
Mr Learn is the video-course / LMS side, powered by an external LMS (Graphy). From the admin
panel the team can: - Browse video courses and manage learners. - Push MAS students into the LMS
as learners. - Sync each student's course progress back into MAS. - Send automatic WhatsApp
reminders to nudge students who are falling behind. - Attach courses to a batch's roadmap.
In one line: Mr Learn = the platform's video courses, with progress tracking and automatic reminders.
How Mr Test & Mr Learn diﬀer: Mr Test = exams; Mr Learn = video lessons. Both pull their
results/progress back into the student's MAS roadmap so everything lives in one place.
Why customers love the platform (talking points)
One platform, whole journey — sales, enrollment, teaching, testing, mentoring, and placement in
a single system.
Structured learning — every student follows a clear weekly roadmap, not a pile of random
content.
Engagement built-in — XP, streaks, and badges keep students coming back and ﬁnishing
courses.
Personalized — students in the same batch can follow diﬀerent plans.
Smart sales — AI calling and predicted-interest help the team convert more leads with less eﬀort.
Nothing falls through the cracks — leads, progress, test results, and reminders are all tracked
automatically.
Mentor + placement focus — built around outcomes, including Pay-After-Placement programs.
Common questions
"Is Mr Test / Mr Learn built in-house?" — They're admin consoles that connect to specialist
external tools (an exam engine and an LMS) and pull all results back into MAS, so the team
manages everything from one place.
"Can two students in a batch learn diﬀerent things?" — Yes. Each student can be assigned their
own course/roadmap.
"How does the AI calling work?" — An AI voice agent ("Aarya") calls leads, and the system predicts
their interest level from the call so reps know who to prioritize.
"What keeps students motivated?" — A full gamiﬁcation system: points, levels, daily streaks, and
achievement badges.
